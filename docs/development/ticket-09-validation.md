## Acceptance gaps

The frozen fixture, scoring contract, capture entry and independent-review workflow are implemented. Synthetic and browser checks do not prove the 95% criterion. The following remain required before ticket 09 can close:

1. An independently calibrated recording of physical screen and actual speaker output (or another apparatus with demonstrated common timing and residual uncertainty).
2. Independent audio/pixel annotations for all 40 frozen expected events, including omissions, ambiguity, extras and intervals near the boundary; original per-event frame/waveform evidence and calibration retained.
3. A scored result meeting the conservative inclusive 200 ms / 95% rule, or measured scheduling corrections with before/after recordings if the result fails. No unmeasured latency compensation has been introduced.
4. Repeat the measurement on final integration, including actual mixed gaze/expression conditions and the separately required full-system checks. An isolated head or graph capture cannot substitute for these.

The tracker and specification were deliberately left unchanged by this implementation. Ticket 09 remains open until actual evidence satisfies every acceptance criterion. See `web/README-measurement.md` for reproducible commands and the annotation schema.
